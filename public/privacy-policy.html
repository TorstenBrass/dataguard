Privacy Policy

DataGuard — Privacy Watchdog
Last updated: May 23, 2026

Overview

DataGuard is a privacy-focused app that helps users understand how other apps handle their personal data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. DataGuard does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier.

Community Reports

DataGuard includes an optional community reporting feature powered by Supabase. If you choose to submit a report about an app's privacy practices, only the app name and your report text are stored. No personal information is attached to submissions.

Analytics & Tracking

DataGuard does not use any analytics services, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. We do not track your usage, location, device information, or browsing behavior.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties. Period.

Payments

DataGuard is free. There is no in-app purchase or payment processor, and the app never collects or stores any payment details.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — hosts community report data (anonymous, no personal info)
  • Vercel — hosts the web version of the app

Children's Privacy

DataGuard does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app contains no user accounts or personal data collection of any kind.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version here with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
